# Managing Risks and Risk Assessment at Work

## Risk-management cycle
HSE presents risk management as a practical cycle: identify hazards, assess risks, control risks, record significant findings, and review controls. Hazard identification should consider work activities, plant and equipment, substances, workplace conditions, non-routine operations, previous incidents, and input from workers.

## Assess and control
For each hazard, consider who may be harmed, how harm could occur, the likely severity and likelihood, existing controls, and further action. Control decisions should consider eliminating the hazard first. If that is not possible, options include redesign, substitution, reduced exposure, practical safe-working measures, and suitable PPE. Actions should have clear ownership and timing.

## Record and review
Significant findings should capture hazards, affected people, and controls. Reviews are needed when controls may no longer be effective, workplace conditions change, workers identify problems, or incidents and near misses show that assumptions or controls need reconsideration.
