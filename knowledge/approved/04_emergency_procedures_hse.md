# Emergency Procedures

## Planning principle
HSE guidance emphasizes planned, recorded and rehearsed arrangements for emergencies that may have wider consequences. Reliable response depends on competent people, realistic practice, clear responsibilities, and coordination where more than one employer shares a workplace.

## Elements of an emergency arrangement
Planning should address alarms, contact with emergency services, safe escape and assembly, emergency lighting and routes, and people authorized to take control. It should identify supporting roles such as incident coordination, first aid, or technical support, and consider essential actions for plant shutdown, energy or substance isolation, and making processes safe. Training should consider workers who may need additional assistance.

## Recovery
Normal work should not resume while serious danger remains. Emergency arrangements should therefore include a controlled transition back to safe operation.
