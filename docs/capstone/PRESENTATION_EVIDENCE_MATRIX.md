## Official story

**Situation:** HSE professionals work across operational records, procedures and safety-critical decisions.

**Complication:** information is fragmented, and generative AI alone may hallucinate or offer action without sufficient evidence.

**Resolution:** YvY combines a controlled agent workflow, scoped tools, approved retrieval, deterministic evidence verification, owned memory and digital human approval. The HSE professional retains field authority; the system makes the software decision path more traceable.
